AGROLATTICE 11.3 — Research Foundation & Predictive Evidence Update
===================================================================
Based on: AGROLATTICE 11.2 — Spatial & Upgrade Integrity Update
Application version: 20.3-release11.3-research-foundation

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Research Evidence Registry
   - Adds an additive models_evidence/research_evidence.sqlite database (schema 1.1.0).
   - Persists datasets, canonical long-form observations, model cards, training runs,
     predictions, uncertainty fields, recommendations, treatment outcomes and external
     benchmark runs.
   - Explicit evidence types distinguish Measured, Derived, Assumption, Prior,
     Forecast and Model output.

2. Agricultural validation framework
   - Adds grouped CV, leave-one-group-out, leave-one-year-out (LOYO),
     leave-one-region-out (LORO), forward/walk-forward evaluation and random CV as a
     diagnostic option.
   - Preprocessing is cloned/fitted inside each training fold.
   - Adds regression/classification metrics, binary calibration tables and an
     applicability/support diagnostic for new cases.

3. Research Model Laboratory
   - Adds leakage-safe CPU baselines: Ridge/Logistic, Random Forest, Extra Trees and
     Histogram Gradient Boosting.
   - Adds optional/runtime-supported XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost and TabPFN backends.
   - Adds model registration, artifact persistence and prediction registration.
   - Stores regression outputs or classification labels/probability maps without
     forcing categorical predictions into a numeric field.
   - Adds data-readiness checks before training so inadequate rows, missing targets,
     poor feature completeness or weak split structure are surfaced before fitting.
   - TabPFN remains optional so AGROLATTICE still starts without it.

4. Environmental Pest & Disease Early Warning
   - Independent adaptation of Wadhwa & Malik (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.compag.2024.109472.
   - Implements Temp_Diff, Hum_Diff, Avg_Hum and Magnus-form VPD engineering.
   - Supports multiple baseline classifiers, SMOTE-ENN inside the training fold,
     agricultural validation protocols, optional CatBoost/Optuna tuning and SHAP.
   - Pest-disease mapping is contextual decision support; predicted pest risk is not a
     confirmed disease diagnosis.

5. Central Phenology Service
   - Adds generic thermal-time crop-stage estimation.
   - Integrates, rather than replaces, the existing Mechanistic Maize Twin.
   - The Release 11.0 Laurent-inspired maize constants and genotype priors are unchanged.
   - Supports phenology evidence/consensus workflows while keeping observed and modeled
     states distinct.

6. External benchmark foundation
   - Adds metadata/adapters for CropNet, YieldSAT and WorldCereal.
   - Large external datasets are not bundled or silently downloaded.
   - Local benchmark snapshots can be registered with source/version/licence/provenance.

7. Multimodal fusion foundation
   - Adds a CPU, validation-weighted modality-aware late-fusion baseline.
   - Separate modality models are evaluated using held-out agricultural folds.
   - Missing modalities are handled per row by renormalizing remaining weights.
   - Inter-modality spread is explicitly labeled model disagreement, not calibrated UQ.

8. Persistent Twin and workspace integration
   - AgroLattice Twin gains a Research evidence view for registered field/trial
     predictions and recommendations; field-linked evidence remains visible when a
     trial is also active and vice versa.
   - Crop Decisions gains Phenology service and Pest early warning.
   - Models & Evidence gains Research registry, Research model lab, Multimodal fusion
     and External benchmarks.
   - Research Registry can separately record proposed recommendations and later
     measured treatment/outcome records, preserving the causal-audit data contract.
   - Country changes clear country-specific research/pest/phenology/fusion session state.

9. Upgrade integrity
   - Safe migration is extended to the Research Evidence DB when present and to
     non-SQLite model artifacts in models_evidence/.
   - Release 11.2 sources with only the three protected legacy DBs remain supported.

DEPENDENCIES
------------

  xgboost, lightgbm, catboost, optuna, imbalanced-learn, shap, packaging

Optional advanced dependency:
  requirements_research_optional.txt -> tabpfn

SCIENTIFIC LIMITS
-----------------
- A model begins as Prototype. Publication performance does not automatically validate it
  for a local field, crop, genotype, season or management regime.
- The applicability score is a robust support diagnostic, not a probability of correctness.
- The multimodal fusion baseline is an AGROLATTICE adaptation and not an exact reproduction
  of the Mena et al. neural gated-fusion network.
- Exact MMST-ViT, PhenoYieldNet, PB-CNN, ALIC, weakly supervised yield, SCM-GAT,
  causal-effect estimators, N optimizer, DRL irrigation, NSGA-II fertilization,
  market GNNs and federated learning remain future modules.
- Mechanistic maize timing does not guarantee pollen quantity, seed set or seed purity.
