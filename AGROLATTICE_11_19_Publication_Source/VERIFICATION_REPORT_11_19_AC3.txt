Focused K-means full-inventory regression
-----------------------------------------
Input: 1,012 locations; 1991-2020; all 12 months; temperature, mean
precipitation, FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration and solar radiation; 80%
completeness; seed 42; candidate k=2-50.

All 1,012 locations and all 80 Oaxaca locations passed the feature-matrix
completeness rule. Raw silhouette reached 0.4430 at k=49, but its smallest zone
contained only four locations and therefore failed the prespecified 2%
minimum-zone safeguard (21 locations). Among eligible solutions, k=2 had the
strongest silhouette (0.3715; smallest zone 355 locations). This confirms why
raw silhouette alone must not select the reported zone count.
