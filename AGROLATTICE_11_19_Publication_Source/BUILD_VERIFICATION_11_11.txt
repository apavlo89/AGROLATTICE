SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES / KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------
- Daily sowing-date output is climate-exposure screening with explicit assumptions, not an official planting calendar or cultivar-specific mechanistic yield optimiser.
- Operational pest inference requires an explicitly eligible registered artifact and predictors that can be reproduced without fabrication; risk is not field confirmation or disease diagnosis.
- Nutrient readiness is a guardrail, not proof that a causal/identifiable response surface exists.
- AquaCrop/DSSAT/APSIM execution success does not establish local calibration, parameter equivalence, cross-season validation or decision benefit.
- Recorded operation costs may not be complete production accounting.
- Chronology in the Crop Decision Timeline does not establish causality.
- The build container does not include Streamlit, so a full interactive browser/widget test was not executed here. RUN_APP.bat performs the runtime dependency/module/schema preflight in the target Windows/Anaconda environment.
