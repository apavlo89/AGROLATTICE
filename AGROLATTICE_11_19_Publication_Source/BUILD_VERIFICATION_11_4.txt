presence/absence labels.

SCIENTIFIC/IMPLEMENTATION LIMITS
--------------------------------
- NASA POWER values are gridded environmental estimates, not local station measurements.
- Exact MMST-ViT, PhenoYieldNet, PB-CNN, ALIC, SCM-GAT, causal treatment-effect estimators,
  EO/crop-model nitrogen optimization, DRL irrigation, NSGA-II fertilizer optimization,
  market GNNs/transformers and federated learning are not claimed as implemented in 11.4.
- Adaptive fusion gate weights are predictive reliability signals, not causal effects.
- Multimodal disagreement is not a calibrated uncertainty interval.
- Hybrid residual correction is promoted only when held-out RMSE improves.
- Weakly supervised fine-scale yield estimates are not independently validated subfield measurements.
- Field geometry remains authoritative for spatial products.
