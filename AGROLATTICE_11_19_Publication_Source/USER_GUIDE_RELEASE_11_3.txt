AGROLATTICE 11.3 USER GUIDE — RESEARCH FOUNDATION
================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Release 11.3 adds a research evidence and predictive-model layer while keeping
AGROLATTICE's persistent spatial field/Twin/experiment architecture authoritative.
A model output never replaces a measured observation merely because it is newer.

1. RESEARCH REGISTRY
--------------------
Open Models & Evidence > Research registry.

Use it to review:
• registered datasets and their source/version/provenance;
• models and their promotion status;
• predictions linked to fields/trials;
• recommendations and later observed outcomes;
• benchmark runs and model cards.

Model statuses are:
Prototype -> Internally validated -> Externally validated -> Operationally eligible -> Retired.
Promotion is a scientific/governance decision. A high random-CV score is not enough.

2. RESEARCH MODEL LAB
---------------------
Open Models & Evidence > Research model lab.
Upload or select a research table, then choose target, predictors and task type.
Prefer a deployment-relevant split:
• LOYO for unseen seasons/years;
• LORO for unseen regions;
• grouped or leave-one-group-out for fields/sites/genotypes/parents;
• forward validation for genuine future forecasting.

Available core models do not need a GPU. XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost are
supported when installed. TabPFN is optional.

All imputation, scaling and encoding are contained in the sklearn pipeline and
are fitted on training folds. A data-readiness panel checks rows, observed targets,
feature completeness and split levels before training. Register only models whose
validation context is understood. When producing new predictions, inspect
applicability/support flags. Classification predictions are stored as class labels
with probability maps where the fitted estimator supplies probabilities; they are
not coerced into fake numeric values.

3. PHENOLOGY SERVICE
--------------------
Open Crop Decisions > Phenology service.

Generic thermal-time mode:
• select/upload daily weather;
• set planting date, base/upper temperature and stage GDD thresholds;
• review accumulated GDD, current stage and next-stage estimate.

Mechanistic maize mode:
• uses the existing Mechanistic Maize Twin and disclosed Laurent-inspired MFS
  equations/priors;
• does not change the Release 11.0 biological constants;
• separates model output from observed flowering/leaf-count evidence.

Use observed field phenology for calibration wherever available.

4. PEST EARLY WARNING
---------------------
Open Crop Decisions > Pest early warning.

The 11.3 environmental workflow independently adapts Wadhwa & Malik (2024).
It can derive:
  Temp_Diff = MaxT - MinT
  Hum_Diff = RH morning - RH evening
  Avg_Hum  = mean of the two RH values
  VPD      = saturation vapour pressure(Tmean) - actual vapour pressure(RHmean)

Select an appropriate target and predictors, then use a grouped/year/region/
forward validation design where possible. SMOTE-ENN, if selected, occurs within
the training-fold pipeline. Optional SHAP explains fitted tree predictions.

Important: predicted pest class/risk does not confirm disease. Pest-disease
associations are contextual warnings and require crop scouting/diagnostic evidence.

5. MULTIMODAL FUSION
--------------------
Open Models & Evidence > Multimodal fusion.
Define at least two non-overlapping feature groups such as:
  weather, EO, soil, terrain, sensors, management.

Each modality receives its own model. Validation RMSE determines baseline fusion
weights. If a modality is entirely missing for a new row, remaining weights are
renormalized. The output includes modality predictions and inter-modality spread.
Do not interpret the spread as Bayesian or calibrated uncertainty.

6. EXTERNAL BENCHMARKS
----------------------
Open Models & Evidence > External benchmarks.
11.3 includes metadata/adapters for CropNet, YieldSAT and WorldCereal, not their
large source data. Obtain datasets under the original terms, keep their licence/
version information, and register the local snapshot. Prefer benchmark-defined
LOYO/LORO or transfer splits where available.

7. TWIN RESEARCH EVIDENCE
-------------------------
Open AgroLattice Twin > Research evidence.
The view surfaces registered predictions/recommendations linked to the active
field/trial identifiers. When both a field and trial are active, evidence linked to
either context is shown rather than requiring both IDs on the same record.
Prototype/Internal models are visibly distinguished from stronger validation
states. The existing Twin remains the persistent system of record for field/season
state.

8. RECOMMENDATION/OUTCOME SEPARATION
------------------------------------
The new registry distinguishes proposed recommendations from what was actually
applied and what outcome was later measured. The Research Registry includes manual
forms for recording a proposed research recommendation and a later observed
treatment/outcome while preserving their separation. This data contract is
deliberately implemented before causal-effect estimators. Do not call an association
causal without a justified causal design.

9. UPGRADE/MIGRATION
--------------------
MIGRATE_USER_DATA_FROM_EXISTING_APP.bat now also migrates the Research Evidence
DB/model artifacts when a newer source contains them. When upgrading from 11.2,
there is no research DB to migrate; the new 11.3 DB remains intact while the three
legacy research/operations DBs are copied from the selected working source.

10. WHAT IS NOT YET IMPLEMENTED
-------------------------------
11.3 is the foundation release. Exact MMST-ViT, PhenoYieldNet, PB-CNN, ALIC,
weakly supervised subfield yield, SCM-GAT, causal estimators, N optimization,
DRL irrigation, NSGA-II fertilizer optimization, price GNN/transformers and
federated learning remain future research modules. They now have a common data,
validation, provenance and deployment interface to plug into safely.
