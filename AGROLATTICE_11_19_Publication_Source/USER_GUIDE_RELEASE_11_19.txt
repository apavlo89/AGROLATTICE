CLIMATE-ZONE DISCOVERY
----------------------
Open Climate & Earth Observation > Climate Zones & Transferability >
Climate-zone discovery.

For most research use, select "Robust multi-method analysis (recommended)".
Choose the period, months and candidate variables, then decide whether the app
should identify a compact variable subset or retain every selected variable.
The advanced section controls the clustering methods, largest zone count,
validation depth, completeness threshold, PCA variance target and optional
density-based diagnostic.

Use "Focused K-means analysis" only when a simpler K-means workflow or direct
comparability with a prespecified analysis is required. It can optimise the
variable subset and zone count or retain researcher-selected variables.

Candidate zone counts can be examined through k=50. Automatic K-means will not
select a solution whose smallest zone contains fewer than 2% of analysed
locations, although such solutions remain in the diagnostic table and can be
requested manually for explicitly exploratory work.

Before analysis, the Mexico publication-reference workspace verifies that the
complete 1,012-location inventory and all 32 administrative regions are present.
After analysis, open Location coverage audit to reconcile source, eligible,
mapped and excluded locations. Do not interpret a geographic gap until this
audit has been checked.

The results separate the selected method, number of climate zones, held-out
separation, resampling stability, assignment confidence and PCA dimensionality.
Inspect the climate-space map, geographic distribution, method comparison,
assignment confidence, robustness, method-assignment flow and zone profiles.
The number of PCA components is not the number of climate zones.

More zones are not automatically better. Results are empirical environmental
groups rather than official agroecological zones. Technical engine identifiers
are retained only in reproducibility metadata and developer documentation.
