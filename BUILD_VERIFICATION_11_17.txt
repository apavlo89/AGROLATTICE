Implemented scope
-----------------
- Dedicated Help Command Centre with Start Here, guided workflows, workspace guides, data-readiness matrix, scientific labels, terminology, troubleshooting, search and release guide.
- Context-aware onboarding state derived from small persisted Field/Experiment/Twin/Model/Report summaries.
- Guided workflows for first field/season, first experiment, maize synchrony, Persistent Twin, train/validate model, decision-to-outcome and reproducible publication.
- Standardised evidence vocabulary: Observed, Recorded, Retrieved, Derived, Mechanistic, ML prediction, Forecast, Scenario, Recommendation, Actual operation, Outcome, Causal estimate.
- Consistent "What do I need here?" panel integrated into Home, Fields & Operations, AgroLattice Twin, Climate & Earth Observation, Crop Decisions, Experiments, Models & Evidence, Reports and Data & Settings.
- Searchable scientific glossary and safe troubleshooting guidance.
- Contextual hover-help engine updated to 10.4.4 with Experimental Unit, Applicability, Forecast and Mechanistic Model terminology.
- Current-release guide, README, changelog, manifest, methods manifest and technical basis updated.

Scientific/data boundary
------------------------
- No new scientific model or paper-derived method is introduced.
- No scientific database schema changes.
- No mechanistic maize equation or parameter prior changed.
- Readiness checks are metadata availability checks, not validation/QC certification.
- Guidance does not automatically retrieve NASA/Sentinel data, execute crop models, train ML models or rebuild the large climate dataset.
