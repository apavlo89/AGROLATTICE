AGROLATTICE 11.10 — CLIMATE & EARTH OBSERVATION COMMAND CENTRE
===============================================================

Release purpose
---------------
Release 11.10 reorganises Climate & Earth Observation around the active mapped
field and turns the workspace into a coherent environmental research workflow.
It preserves the established 19-variable country climate dataset, existing
climate-comparison/risk/spatial methods and Sentinel-2 processing while making
field context, data acquisition, evidence provenance and navigation clearer.

New Climate & EO Command Centre
--------------------------------
- New lightweight landing view with Environmental Pulse for the active field.
- Persistent workspace navigation:
  Overview | Field Climate | Climate Comparison | Spatial & Transferability |
  Climate Risk | Earth Observation | Evidence & Data.
- Weather, EO, root-zone and sensor freshness are shown from saved evidence;
  entering the page does not trigger remote retrieval or model execution.
- Active field/research-centre/crop/season context is carried into the workspace.
- Quick navigation to Research Data Hub, Twin and Fields & Operations.

Field Climate Explorer
----------------------
- New 19-variable explorer over the installed canonical agroclimate dataset.
- Variables grouped into Temperature, Water & atmospheric demand,
  Radiation & cloud, Soil energy, and Wind & pressure.
- Monthly selected-year vs long-term climatology with variability context.
- Annual trend view and year × month heatmap.
- Coverage and source information remain visible.
- When a mapped field is active, the nearest installed climate reference is
  selected by field centroid and explicitly labelled as gridded/location
  evidence rather than an on-field station measurement.
- Local field-sensor observations are displayed separately; they are not
  silently merged with gridded climate values.

Direct environmental data acquisition
-------------------------------------
- Added explicit one-click NASA POWER weather update for a mapped field.
- Retrieves the canonical 19-variable daily weather bundle already supported by
  the Research Data Hub.
- Uses authoritative field centroid and a defensible season/date window:
  structured sowing/harvest dates when available, otherwise recorded season
  year, otherwise a rolling 12-month period.
- No crop date is invented.
- Retrieval provenance is saved to Research Evidence and compatible linked Twin
  weather is updated when a Twin exists.
- Added explicit Quick update EO for active mapped fields using the existing
  Sentinel-2 L2A processing system with provider failover and quality masking.
- EO results are saved to Research Evidence and may update a compatible linked
  Twin satellite record.
- Neither remote operation runs automatically on page entry.

Climate comparison & transferability polish
-------------------------------------------
- Climate similarity can now use the active mapped field as the reference
  environment through its nearest installed climate location.
- The UI states that this is a gridded/location proxy and not a field weather
  measurement.
- Existing similarity, explanation, robustness, analogue and spatial-climate
  methods are preserved rather than replaced.
- Spatial & Transferability includes clearer scientific guidance: PCA remains
  the most interpretable default, while nonlinear embeddings are not presented
  as literal agronomic distance.

Earth Observation polish
------------------------
- Satellite Monitoring is presented as Earth Observation and automatically
  preloads the authoritative active-field geometry when available.
- Legacy internal "Module A / Module B" wording was replaced with researcher-
  facing crop-development, root-zone, phenology/stress and evidence language.
- Satellite navigation was simplified to:
  Field status & time series | Imagery | Seasonal anomalies |
  Phenology & stress | Advanced methods.
- Structured field-season sowing dates may prepopulate EO context; AGROLATTICE
  does not fabricate a sowing date from the season year.
- Full manual AOI/search/processing controls remain available for advanced use.

Evidence & provenance
---------------------
- Field-linked weather/EO acquisitions and Persistent Twin environmental
  attachments are visible from one Evidence & Data view.
- Researchers can explicitly persist current session climate/EO analyses as a
  JSON evidence snapshot registered in the Research Evidence database.
- Saving an analysis does not upgrade a similarity score, EO index, anomaly or
  heuristic risk result into causal or field-validated agronomic evidence.

Performance
-----------
- Release 11.10 reuses the process-cached climate table introduced in 11.6.
- A small climate-location catalogue is constructed once per country runtime and
  reused for field-reference selectors, avoiding repeated de-duplication of the
  multi-million-row climate table.
- Field Climate caches only the selected location subset by dataset signature.
- Remote NASA/STAC work, similarity, risk, PCA and raster processing remain
  explicit actions/views rather than running on workspace entry.

Polish / maintenance
--------------------
- Fixed the shared workspace header that could incorrectly label Climate & EO as
  "Field Command Centre".
- Fixed release-artifact sorting so 11.10 is correctly treated as newer than
  11.9 (numeric rather than lexicographic ordering).
- Help now points to the 11.10 user guide.
- RUN_APP.bat now preflights the 11.10 Climate & EO module and the cached climate
  location catalogue.

Database / dependency status
----------------------------
- No database schema migration in 11.10.
- Field Operations, Maize Pollination, Persistent Twin and Research Evidence
  SQLite files are not intentionally modified by this release.
- No new mandatory dependency was added.

Scientific limitations
----------------------
- NASA/gridded climate values are environmental estimates, not equivalent to a
  local station or field sensor.
- Nearest installed climate location is a transparent spatial proxy; climate
  similarity does not establish agronomic equivalence or causal transferability.
- Sentinel-2 indices depend on cloud masking, usable pixels, scene availability
  and spatial support; small plots may be mixed-pixel observations.
- Hazard/risk outputs must not be interpreted as validated yield effects unless
  a validated crop-response model supports that inference.
- Full interactive Streamlit browser testing must be performed in the user's
  Windows/Anaconda installation because Streamlit is unavailable in the build
  container.
