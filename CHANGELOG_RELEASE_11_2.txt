AGROLATTICE 11.2 - Spatial & Upgrade Integrity Update

- Existing-trial Spatial linkage no longer crashes on a missing exact_match status key.
- Trial spatial re-linking is validated before commit; a field/boundary change is blocked
  if any saved treatment unit would lie outside the proposed trial boundary.
- Exact mapped-field mode now uses the authoritative mapped-field geometry explicitly.

  exact trial name at the database layer as well as the UI.
